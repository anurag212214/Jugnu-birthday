Dear Jugnu, ❤️

Today is your day, but somehow I feel like I am the lucky one for having you as a part of my story.

When I look back at everything we've shared, I don't just see pictures. I see little moments, conversations, smiles, journeys, silly things, and memories that became special simply because they were with you.

Mujhe humari baarish mein bheegne wali yaad yaad hai… 🌧️
Saath mein mandir jaana yaad hai… 🛕
Banaras ki woh yaadein, Patna ki woh roads, saath chalna, aur woh chhote-chhote moments jo dheere-dheere humari story ka hissa ban gaye.

Kabhi socha nahi tha ki kisi ke saath bitaya hua ek ordinary sa moment bhi itna special ho sakta hai.

Humari journey hamesha perfect nahi rahi. Kabhi arguments hue, kabhi misunderstandings hui, kabhi things difficult bhi hui… lekin un sabke beech jo achhe moments the, unki value mere liye kabhi kam nahi hogi.

Jab main peeche mudkar dekhta hoon, toh mujhe regrets se zyada memories dikhai deti hain. ❤️

Tum meri story ka ek bahut khoobsurat chapter rahi ho, Jugnu.

Mujhe nahi pata aage life humein kis direction mein le jayegi, aur shayad har cheez ka answer aaj milna bhi zaroori nahi hai.

Bas itna jaanta hoon ki jo waqt humne saath bitaya, jo memories humne banayi, aur jo smiles ek-doosre ko di… main un sabke liye genuinely grateful hoon.

Aaj bas ek wish hai—

Tum hamesha khush raho.
Tumhare dreams poore hon.
Tum apni life mein bahut aage jao.
Aur tumhare face ki woh smile hamesha bani rahe, jo tumhe aur bhi beautiful bana deti hai. ✨

Aur haan…

Agar kabhi life ke kisi random moment mein baarish ho, Banaras ya Patna ki koi road yaad aaye, ya koi purani memory achanak smile de jaaye…

toh bas itna yaad rakhna—

Kahin na kahin, kisi ki favourite memories mein tum bhi ho. ❤️

Thank you for being a part of my journey, Jugnu.
